Carpark SG

As an occasional driver, I am unsure of the places where experienced drivers usually park their vehicles at. Usually I would just park at the destination’s carpark. However, sometimes there are no available parking lots and I would have to drive around aimlessly searching for other parking lots. As a result, I might be late for the appointment and also wasted petrol searching.

Research and Analysis

A search on the iOS app store results in a couple of apps related to carparks. A notable one is “Carpark Rates” published by sgCarMart. This mobile app has many carparks, however it does not include the public carparks like those from HDB. Many clicks are also required to see the nearby carparks on a map. This makes it difficult to use the app since the user should be focusing on driving. There are also a limited number of carparks that has lot availability information.
